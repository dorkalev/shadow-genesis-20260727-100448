# People Security Policy

Personnel are attracted, developed, and retained with the competence to meet control responsibilities. **Background checks** are performed where legally permitted before granting access to customer data, recorded under `evidence/people/<person>/background-check.md`. **Security training** (`policies/security-training.md`) is completed at onboarding and annually; completion is recorded per person. **Competence**: role responsibilities are defined in `roles-and-responsibilities.md`; the owner self-attests relevant competence and engages advisors where gaps exist. An annual review evaluates whether the team has the skills to operate the controls.
