# Service Objectives & Commitments


Principal service commitments to customers: keep customer data confidential, available, and processed with integrity; deploy only reviewed, tested changes; notify customers of material security incidents. These objectives are specified with enough clarity to identify risks against them (see `risk-management.md`). System requirements to meet them are enforced by the change gates and cloud controls.
