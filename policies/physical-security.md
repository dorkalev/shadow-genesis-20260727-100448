# Physical & Endpoint Security Policy

> Draft authored by the shadow. Review and approve by merging; that merge is the approval record. Until approved, this credits as *implemented*, not *verified*.

The company operates no data centers; physical and environmental controls for the production runtime are **inherited from the cloud provider as a sub-service organization** (carved out in the system description; provider SOC 2 reviewed annually — `evidence/subservice/`). Company endpoints (laptops) must have full-disk encryption, screen-lock, and be inventoried; posture is attested per device. Disposal follows the retention policy.
