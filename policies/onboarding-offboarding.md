# Onboarding & Offboarding Policy


**Onboarding:** create the least-privilege grant set for the role, verify MFA enrollment, assign policy acknowledgment and security training, record in `evidence/onboarding/<person>.md`, and schedule a day-14 access verification. Background checks are performed where legally permitted and recorded. **Offboarding:** on the last day, revoke every grant enumerated in the access register, capture a before/after report, and file it under `evidence/lifecycle/`.
