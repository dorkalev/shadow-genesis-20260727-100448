# Roles and Responsibilities

> Draft authored by the shadow. Review and approve by merging; that merge is the approval record. Until approved, this credits as *implemented*, not *verified*.

For a solo/very-small team, the owner holds all control roles and this concentration is a disclosed limitation compensated by branch protection, immutable archives, and automated review. Roles: **Owner/Founder** — accountable for security, approves policies and releases, performs quarterly reviews. **Independent review** — an automated agent (disclosed compensating control, not human segregation). Reporting line: all → Owner. Authorities and the access each role receives are defined in `policies/role-matrix.md`.
