# Information Security Policy


This is the umbrella policy. The company protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer data through the controls described in this pack and enforced in code (see `sdlc/SDLC.md` in the shadow install). Scope: all repositories, cloud resources, SaaS accounts, and endpoints. Every person with access agrees to this pack (see the Acceptable Use Policy). Policies are reviewed at least annually (`review_by` frontmatter) and re-approved by the owner.
