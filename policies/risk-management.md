# Risk Management Policy


Risk is assessed at least annually and on significant change. The assessment identifies risks to the service objectives, rates likelihood × impact (1–4), considers fraud explicitly (CC3.3), and records a treatment (accept / mitigate / transfer / avoid) with an owner. Mitigations are tracked as issues. Significant changes (new vendor, new data type, architecture change) trigger a re-assessment. The living register is `policies/risk-register.md`; assessment minutes are filed under `evidence/risk-assessment-YYYY/`.
