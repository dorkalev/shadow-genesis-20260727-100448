# Code of Conduct


All personnel and contractors act with integrity and are accountable for their internal-control responsibilities. Prohibited: sharing credentials, disabling security controls, bypassing the change gates without the documented hotfix procedure, and mishandling customer data. Fraud, misuse of access, and circumvention of controls are grounds for removal. Concerns are reported via a private issue or directly to the owner. Accountability is enforced structurally: every change is attributed to an authenticated identity, gated, and archived.
