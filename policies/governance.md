# Governance & Oversight

For a solo/owner-operated company there is no independent board; this is a **disclosed limitation**. Oversight is exercised by the owner through a documented quarterly internal-control review that examines: the compliance gauge trend, open deficiencies (the shadow's auto-opened tickets), incidents, bypass merges, access-review results, and risk-register changes. Where an advisor or investor is engaged, they receive the quarterly summary and their feedback is recorded. Minutes of each review are filed under `evidence/oversight/<quarter>/`. Compensating controls for the absent independent board: enforced branch protection, immutable change archives, automated review, and release confirmation.
