# Vendor Management Policy

> Draft authored by the shadow. Review and approve by merging; that merge is the approval record. Until approved, this credits as *implemented*, not *verified*.

Vendors with access to customer data are assessed before use (inherent risk, data accessed), covered by a DPA/terms, and their SOC 2 (or equivalent) report reviewed. LLM providers are treated as data-processing vendors. Vendors are re-reviewed annually; the living list is `policies/vendor-register.md`. Sub-service organizations (cloud, GitHub) are carved out in the system description with their reports on file.
