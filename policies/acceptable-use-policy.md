# Acceptable Use Policy

Company systems (GitHub, cloud, SaaS, endpoints) are used only for authorized business purposes. Users must: enable MFA on every account, keep endpoints encrypted and screen-locked, never commit secrets, and use only approved vendors for customer data. Acknowledgment of this pack is required at onboarding and annually.

## AI / LLM use
AI coding agents and LLM tools may be used for development and review, subject to
`ai-agent-use.md`. Only **Public** or **Internal** data may be sent to an LLM vendor; secrets and
**Confidential**/customer personal data must never be pasted into a prompt or tool. Approved LLM
providers are inventoried as sub-processors with DPAs (`vendor-register.md`, `evidence/vendors/`).
