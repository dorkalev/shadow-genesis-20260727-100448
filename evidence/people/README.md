# People Evidence (CC1.1, CC1.4, CC6.2)

Per-person records that require a **real human action** and therefore must be completed and
signed by the person — the shadow does not and will not fabricate these. Files here are
templates awaiting the owner's genuine completion; until signed with a real date, the
corresponding criteria correctly credit as *not fully in place*.

Contents:
- `policy-acknowledgment-2026.md` — annual acknowledgment of the policy pack.
- `security-training-2026.md` — annual security-awareness training completion.
- `background-check-2026.md` — background check / documented risk-acceptance for the founder.

New joiners: add a row within 2 weeks of start (onboarding checklist,
`policies/onboarding-offboarding.md`).
